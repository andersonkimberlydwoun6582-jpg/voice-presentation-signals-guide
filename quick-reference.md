# Voice signal quick reference

| Signal | What it means | Useful question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pitch | Estimated highness or lowness of voiced speech | Where does my comfortable speaking voice sit today? |
| Pitch variation | How much pitch moves over the sample | Does my speech have natural rises and falls? |
| Brightness | Rough amount of higher-frequency energy | Does the recording sound forward, clear, warm, or dark? |
| Privacy | How the sample is handled | Is my audio staying local or being uploaded? |

Try a private browser-based check: [Voice Gender Test](https://voicegendertest.com/).
